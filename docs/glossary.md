# T036 – PS-012

## Health Insurance Cashless Pre-Authorisation and Settlement Engine

### Glossary of Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Policy** | An insurance contract that provides health coverage to registered members. |
| **Policyholder** | The person who owns or purchases the insurance policy. |
| **Member** | A person covered under a policy who can make an insurance claim. |
| **Sum Insured (SI)** | The maximum insurance coverage amount available under a policy. |
| **Remaining Sum Insured** | The portion of the Sum Insured that is still available after claims have been processed. |
| **Plan** | The policy coverage category: SILVER, GOLD or PLATINUM. |
| **Inception Date** | The date on which the insurance policy starts. |
| **Pre-existing Disease (PED)** | A medical condition that existed before the policy began. |
| **Waiting Period** | A specified period during which certain medical claims are not eligible for coverage. |
| **Specified Illness** | A disease or medical condition with a special waiting period defined by the policy. |
| **Diagnosis Code** | A code used to identify a patient's medical condition or diagnosis. |
| **ICD Chapter** | A category used to group related medical diagnoses. |
| **Network Hospital** | A hospital authorised to provide cashless treatment under the insurance network. |
| **Hospital Tariff** | A schedule of charges or rates applicable at a hospital. |
| **Room Category** | The type of hospital room selected for treatment. |
| **Room Rent Limit** | The maximum room rent covered by the insurance policy per day. |
| **ICU** | Intensive Care Unit; a specialised hospital unit with a separate room rent limit. |
| **Proportionate Deduction** | A reduction applied to associated medical charges when the selected room rent exceeds the policy limit. |
| **Disease Sub-limit** | The maximum amount payable for a specific disease or medical procedure. |
| **Co-pay** | The percentage of an eligible claim amount that must be paid by the customer. |
| **Non-payables** | Hospital expenses that are not covered by the insurance policy. |
| **Consumable Cover Rider** | An additional policy benefit that covers eligible consumable expenses that would otherwise be non-payable. |
| **Pre-authorisation (Pre-auth)** | An initial request from a hospital for insurance approval before or during treatment. |
| **Enhancement** | A request for additional insurance approval after the initial pre-authorisation. |
| **Final Bill** | The final hospital bill submitted after treatment or discharge. |
| **Settlement** | The process of calculating and paying the final eligible insurance amount. |
| **Customer Share** | The amount of the final bill that must be paid by the customer. |
| **Restoration** | Replenishment of exhausted Sum Insured when the policy conditions allow it. |
| **No-Claim Bonus (NCB)** | Additional coverage earned for claim-free policy years. |
| **Query** | A request for additional information before a pre-authorisation decision can be finalised. |
| **Claim Decision Letter** | A report showing the claim status, approved amount, deductions, co-pay and applicable rule IDs. |
| **Policy Utilisation Statement** | A report showing how much of the policy's available coverage has been used and remains. |
| **TPA (Third Party Administrator)** | An organisation that processes and administers health insurance claims on behalf of an insurer. |
